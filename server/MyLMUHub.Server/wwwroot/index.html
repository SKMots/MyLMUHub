mylmuhub

A free pitwall dashboard and personal stats hub for Le Mans Ultimate. One Windows app, no SimHub. Made for endurance racers, and for watching your team-mate drive.

Log in Download for Windows
Race ControlA full-screen dashboard for your second monitor, built from widgets: leaderboard, relative, fuel and virtual energy, tyres, damage, track map, weather, race prediction.
Race paceYour pace on every track from the game's own result files, your level against the alien times, what to work on next, turn by turn.
StrategyPlan an endurance race from the sessions you drove, follow it live with your real lap times and VE, and review it afterwards.
Your teamTeam races found and shared by themselves, a review of every race, everyone's race pace per track and car. On your phone too.
My LMU Hub is open source (MIT). Not affiliated with Studio 397 or Le Mans Ultimate.
