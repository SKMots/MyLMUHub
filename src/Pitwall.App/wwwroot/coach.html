AI Coach

Your own AI reads a summary of a session and writes a debrief with a practice plan, or answers your question. It uses your API key, only when you click.

Before you start: how the AI Coach works

What it does. The app reads your sessions (the game's result files), works out the numbers itself and sends a short summary to the AI you choose. The AI writes a debrief and a plan for your next session, or answers a question about it.

Your own API key. You add a key from Google (Gemini), OpenAI or Anthropic (Claude). My LMU Hub has no AI and no server of its own: the request goes straight from this PC to that provider, with your key.

Only when you click. Nothing is ever sent by itself or in the background. Before each request you see roughly how big it is, and you can read exactly what is sent. Every answer is saved on this PC: opening it again costs nothing.

What is sent. Lap and sector times, track, car, class, session type and date, your finishing position, where on the track you lose time and, there, your braking point, speed and full-throttle point. Not your driver names, not your files, nothing else from your PC.

A free key (Google AI Studio has one) costs nothing but has daily limits. On free tiers a provider may use what you send to improve its models: see its terms.

A paid key is billed by the provider to your account, per token (a token is about ¾ of a word). A debrief is about 1,000 to 2,000 tokens in and out together; with a small model that is usually less than one cent. The provider sets the prices and they differ per model: check its price list, and set a spending limit in its dashboard. Note: a ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro or Gemini subscription is not an API key; API use is billed apart from it.

Your key stays here. It is stored encrypted for your Windows user on this PC (%AppData%\MyLMUHub\coach.json), is never shown again and goes only to that provider. Remove it here at any time.

The AI can be wrong. It only knows these numbers: no steering, gears or tyre data. Take its advice as ideas to try, not facts.
