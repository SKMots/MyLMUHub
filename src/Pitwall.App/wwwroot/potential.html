Weakness ranking

From your result files: dry laps, current game version, counted laps only (no pit, out or slow laps); per track your fastest car there. Possible gain per lap = how much faster your average lap could be: the gap to your optimal lap (your best three sectors: you already drove them, just not in one lap) plus how far the track is behind your level on your other tracks. The order takes the lap length into account: 2 s on a 4-minute Le Mans lap counts for less than 2 s on a 1:40 lap. Usual = the middle (median) of your counted laps in your last 5 sessions there. Tracks behind your level

Your race pace on each track as % of the alien race pace, against your level now.
Your level now = the middle (median) of your race pace % over the tracks you drove in the last 30 days. Gain = how far a track is behind that, per lap. A track you last drove before that is often behind only because you got faster since: going back there is the quickest gain (STALE). Dry laps of all session types that count (no pit, out or slow laps), current game version, tracks in the reference sheet.

Time left on the table

Your best lap against your optimal lap (your best three sectors put together): time you already drove, just not in one lap. And your race pace against your best lap: consistency.
