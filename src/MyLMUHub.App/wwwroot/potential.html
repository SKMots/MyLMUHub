What do these numbers mean?From your result files: dry laps, current game version, counted laps only (no pit, out or slow laps); per track your fastest car there. Possible gain per lap = how much faster your average lap could be: the gap to your optimal lap (your best three sectors: you already drove them, just not in one lap) plus how far the track is behind your level on your other tracks.
