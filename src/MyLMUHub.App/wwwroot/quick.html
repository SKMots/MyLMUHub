The race

--
A timed race ends on the lap you are on when the clock runs out (laps = race time / lap time, rounded up; a stop costs laps). Spare laps are kept in the tank at the end of every stint. With stops the car starts full and the last stop adds only what the finish needs (a shorter stop). Pit window = the laps where that stop still works.
