▸ Team races from your files

Your special events of the last 60 days (the game's scheduled endurance events, solo or with team-mates; daily races are not team races). Each is matched to a team by the car's team name, the team's earlier races and team-mates in the car, and sent to it by itself: when the app starts, every 10 minutes, and when you join a team. A race the team already has (sent by a team-mate) is not added twice.
